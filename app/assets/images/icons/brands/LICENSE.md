# Brand icon provenance

The SVGs in this directory are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons),
vendored from the [`simple-icons` npm package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons)
version **15.22.0** (`icons/<slug>.svg`, unmodified).

Simple Icons releases its icon collection under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) public
domain dedication; see the `LICENSE.md` shipped in that package. The depicted
logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Version 15.22.0 was the newest release still shipping `openai.svg` (OpenAI and
Slack were removed in 16.0.0). `openaigym` was skipped deliberately.

## LobeHub icons

Six monochrome SVGs are from the [`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` npm package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg)
version **1.95.0** (`icons/<name>.svg`, unmodified), published by LobeHub from
the [lobe-icons repository](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) under the
[MIT license](LICENSE-lobehub.md):

- `microsoft.svg`
- `azure.svg`
- `aws.svg`
- `xai.svg`
- `grok.svg`
- `deepseek.svg`

These fill the gaps Simple Icons 15.22.0 could not cover: `microsoft`,
`amazonaws`, `xai`, and `deepseek` are absent from that release. Amazon
(retail) exists in neither source and has no vendored icon. The depicted
logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.
