# Brand icon provenance

The SVGs in this directory are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons),
vendored from the [`simple-icons` npm package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons)
version **15.22.0** (`icons/<slug>.svg`, unmodified).

Simple Icons releases its icon collection under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) public
domain dedication; see the `LICENSE.md` shipped in that package. The depicted
logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Version 15.22.0 was the newest release still shipping `openai.svg` (OpenAI and
Slack were removed in 16.0.0). These requested slugs are absent from 15.22.0
and were skipped: `microsoft`, `amazon`, `amazonaws`, `xai`, `deepseek`
(`openaigym` was skipped deliberately).
